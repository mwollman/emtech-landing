What We Do

Embedded Engineering Support.
Full Lifecycle.

From R&D through deployment — hands-on technician support backed by senior engineering depth, embedded directly in your environment, accountable to your KPIs.

01

R&D & Lab Support

Prototype assembly, HIL rigs, and test automation — built to iterate fast and transfer cleanly into production environments. Embedded lab teams that keep your R&D moving.

02

Testing & Validation

End-to-end test lifecycle support — from test plan development through root-cause analysis. Mechanical, electrical, firmware, and system-level performance evaluation.

03

EOAT & Mechanical Engineering

Custom end-of-arm tooling, gripper design, mechanical integration, and workcell commissioning — engineered to your robot, your payload, and your cycle-time requirements.

04

Field Deployment & Live Ops

Multi-site commissioning, ramp support, and SLA-backed steady-state operations. From first power-on to full throughput across all 50 states and Europe.
